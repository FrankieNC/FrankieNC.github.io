I contribute to [**Formal Conjectures**](https://google-deepmind.github.io/formal-conjectures/), an open-source repository maintained by **Google DeepMind** collecting formalised statements of open mathematical conjectures in [Lean 4](https://leanprover.github.io/lean4/doc/whatIsLean.html), built on [Mathlib](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4).

I was invited to a workshop in London, hosted by Google, dedicated to expanding **Google DeepMind's** Formal Conjectures repository, organised by [Professor Kevin Buzzard](https://www.imperial.ac.uk/people/k.buzzard).
