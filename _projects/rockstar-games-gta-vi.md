From September to December 2025 I worked as a Data Scientist intern in the Player Insights & Analytics team at [Rockstar Games](https://www.rockstargames.com/) in London. I am honoured to be credited in [Grand Theft Auto VI](https://www.rockstargames.com/VI/credits#special-thanks).

Interns at Rockstar design and propose their own projects. For mine, I built a neural network that learns from how people play, to help designers understand player behaviour and decide how to improve the game. I tested several existing machine learning approaches, found that none were suitable, and developed a model of my own based on the research literature. I took the project from the initial idea through to deployment, including the data pipelines needed to train on large volumes of gameplay data.

I am very grateful to my supervisor Tomi Ihalainen and my manager [Nathan Bice](https://www.linkedin.com/in/nathan-bice) for their guidance and trust, and to everyone in the Player Insights & Analytics team for making me feel so welcome. Thank you also for your support with my PhD application.
